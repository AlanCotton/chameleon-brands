Meet Jane. Hummingbird is led by Jane, a successful entrepreneur with over 30 years of experience founding and scaling businesses. She founded HCA – one of the UK's first new-business agencies – and is widely credited with creating an entirely new sector, not just a business. That recognition led the UK government to commission her to author a paper on the future of small business for The Parliamentary Review.

A business mentor and go-to-market strategist with a proven track record of driving revenue, profit and performance, Jane pairs candid one-to-one coaching with a female-only network of senior expertise – so ambitious founders scale sustainably, build high-performing cultures, sharpen their commercial story and win more business.
